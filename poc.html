The product

Every feature built to reward who climbs hardest.

Create Mountains

Launch creative mountains with photos, videos, or audio. Set the rules, pick a category, and watch climbers join.

Compete & Climb

Submit your best entries, vote on others, and climb to the top. Earn achievements and aura.

Community & Friends

Create your own community starting from a mountain, make friends, and engage with them.

Global Leaderboards

Track your ranking by country or globally. See who's the best climber in your region.

Badges & Achievements

Unlock the rarest badges in the app and scale them from Bronze to Diamond. Collect achievements as you compete and create.

Aura

Any action you take in Climby can either increase or decrease your aura. Build your reputation through every move.
